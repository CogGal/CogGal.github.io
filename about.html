Current projects

Fluctuating naturalistic perception

Which parts of understanding speech need our attention? I combine EEG, MEG and intracranial recordings with measures of context and prediction taken from large language models, and track how they change when attention drifts away from listening.

Semantic memory

What makes one thought follow another? I build computational models of free word association that predict what people will say next, and relate the model’s parameters to differences between people.

Past projects

My PhD asked what the brain does with information that never reaches awareness, and whether this unconscious processing is rigid or flexible and goal-directed. I studied it in hearing, where speech goes unnoticed because attention is busy elsewhere or the mind wanders, rather than in the weak or masked visual stimuli the field usually relies on. The work combined behavioral experiments, EEG, and computational measures of meaning and prediction from language models.

Teaching and mentoring

I am passionate about mentoring. I believe our personal contribution to science is unlikely to match that of the people we help along the way.

At the Hebrew University I have taught statistics to undergraduate and graduate students, and courses on the brain and on how non-conscious processes turn into conscious thinking. Since 2022 I have been a statistical advisor to faculty and graduate students in the Department of Psychology, and since 2024 in the School of Education as well. With my PhD advisors, I co-supervised nine BA seminar papers.
