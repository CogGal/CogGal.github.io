Postdoctoral fellow at the Edmond & Lily Safra Center for Brain Sciences, Hebrew University of Jerusalem, in Leon Deouell’s lab.

Research

I am a cognitive scientist broadly interested in “how things come to mind”, or how the brain curates day-to-day conscious experience. I am using behavioral, computational, and electrophysiological methods to study this question through the realms of memory and perception.
