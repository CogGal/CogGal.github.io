Current projects

  • PerceptionWhich parts of understanding speech need our attention? I combine EEG, MEG and intracranial recordings with measures of context and prediction taken from large language models, and track how they change when attention drifts away from listening. With Leon Deouell
  • MemoryWhat makes one thought follow another? I build computational models of free word association that predict what people will say next, and relate the model’s parameters to differences between people. With Ran Hassin
